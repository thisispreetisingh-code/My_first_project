    Step 4 · Copy the format, not the video

    Remix Playbook

    Top creators all borrow formats from each other. The ones who grow add something the original didn't have. Use this to turn a video that's working for someone else into your own, better version.

    1. Find a proven video

      Pick an outlier: a video with 3× more views than that channel's average.

    2. Decode why it worked

      Note the topic, title pattern, thumbnail idea, hook and pacing.

    3. Choose your motive

      Reach, ad revenue, affiliate, product or brand deals. It decides your CTA and length.

    4. Pick 1–2 improvement levers

      Localise, update, test it yourself, simplify, go deeper.

    5. Make it 100% yours

      Your script, footage, voice and thumbnail. Then compare results after 48 hours.
