## Creator Niche Lab (`creator-niche-lab/`)

A research and planning app for new YouTube and Instagram creators. Open `creator-niche-lab/index.html` in a browser. There's no build step and nothing to install.

1. **Niches**: the five niches that perform well and can pay within 30–90 days, with RPM, platform fit, payout paths and a 90-day plan.
2. **Channels**: 27 top channels to study, with why each one works, the format to borrow, and how to improve on it.
3. **Content Studio**: titles, scripts, captions, descriptions, hashtags and keywords for your topic, plus an idea board.
4. **Remix Playbook**: turn a proven video into your own improved version without breaking reused-content rules.
5. **Analyze my channel**: health score, what to fix, and an estimate of the days left until YPP or Instagram monetization.

All research data is in `creator-niche-lab/data.js`; edit it to add niches or channels.
